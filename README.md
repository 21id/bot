# 21ID Telegram Bot
[21ID Telegram bot](https://t.me/id_21bot) is 21ID interface in Telegram, to 
register and verify students, allowing community admins to add verified students to 
their chats and students to search and contact each other (via in-bot notification 
system)  


### About 21ID
21ID is an identification system, developed by Tashkent and Samarkand campus 
students - to make process of finding other peers and joining School 21 communities 
easier
